The Reframe

St. Lucia does not have a deforestation crisis. It has an organic waste crisis. Half of everything buried at Deglos is food scraps, market waste, green waste, and manure — decomposing into methane, a greenhouse gas 28× more potent than CO₂. BiogasSTLucia converts that waste liability into clean cooking gas, agricultural fertilizer, and verified carbon credits. The landfill problem becomes a resource recovery economy.

Calculation Methodology — Gold Standard ACDM

// Pathway 1 — Methane Avoidance from Organic Waste Diversion (ACDM) ER_CH4_avoided = Waste_organic × MCF × DOC × DOCf × F × 16/12 × GWP_CH4 // Pathway 2 — Synthetic Fertilizer Displacement ER_fertilizer = Slurry_output × EF_fertilizer × fNRB // Total Annual Emission Reduction ER_total = (ER_CH4_avoided + ER_fertilizer) × (1 − Leakage) // Default parameters (St. Lucia): MCF = 0.8 (managed landfill), DOC = 0.15 (food waste), DOCf = 0.5, F = 0.5, GWP_CH4 = 28 (AR5), Leakage = 10% default // Sample (1 tonne organic waste diverted per year): ER_CH4 = 1.0 × 0.8 × 0.15 × 0.5 × 0.5 × 1.333 × 28 ≈ 1.12 tCO₂e/tonne diverted // Household diverting 2 tonnes/year: → ER_total ≈ 2.24 tCO₂e/unit/year
